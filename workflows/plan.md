# Plan My Training

The rules that stay the same are in [training-system.md](../context/training-system.md). The workouts themselves are in the tracker, four weeks at a time. Write them as in [tracker.md](tracker.md).

## Training system

`training-system.md` uses these sections, in this order. Match the example's level of detail: specific numbers, no theory.

1. **The workouts:** a table of up to four workout types (A to D) with their main exercises, their day, and the gap before the same muscles are worked again. Then the week, day by day, rest days included.
2. **Rest:** between sets, between hard sessions for the same muscles, and the most training days in a row.
3. **When to step up:** when an exercise moves up, how hard each set should be, and a progression ladder that fits my equipment. With limited weights at home, the ladder goes more reps, then heavier, then slower lowering, then a harder variation. With a full gym, it's more reps, then the smallest weight jump.
4. **Each session:** warm-up, main workout, and cool-down, with times.
5. **Cardio and daily life:** cardio, food, and sleep, fit to my goal.
6. **Logging and adjusting:** when I weigh in, and how a week's review turns into next week.

Fit it to my goal:

* **Lose weight:** 2–4 strength days to keep muscle, cardio 3–5 times a week, a modest calorie deficit with enough protein. The weekly weigh-in matters most.
* **Build muscle:** 3–5 strength days, mostly 8–15 reps with sets ending 1–3 reps short of failure, a small calorie surplus, enough protein. Weight should go up slowly.
* **Get stronger:** compound lifts first, 3–6 reps on the main lifts, 2–3 minutes of rest, weight added in small steps.
* **Move more:** 2–3 full-body days plus cardio. Easy to stick with matters more than anything else.

For a beginner, start easier than they think they can handle. The first week is for learning the moves.

## Plan four weeks

The first plan starts the coming Monday, or today if I want to start right away. Later plans start the Monday after the last planned week, and build on how the last month went: what I finished, my notes, the weekly reviews, and my weight against the goal in my [profile](../context/profile.md).

* Add one workout per training day. Title it by what it trains, like "Push + Core", and give it the type (A to D) from the training system. Use the same title for the same type every week.
* List each workout's exercises with sets and target reps. Put the weight or variation in the name, like "Goblet squats (8 kg)".
* Build up across the weeks. Week 1 learns the moves with reps to spare. Later weeks add reps or weight, following the progression rules.
* Write a week note for each week: its focus, cardio, effort, and progression.
* If those weeks already have workouts, because I used the tracker's "Plan <month> from <month>" button, update them instead of adding more.

Then tell me the plan in a few lines. For a new month, say what changed and why.

## Weekly review

Usually on Sunday, after I weigh in.

1. Read the week: each workout's status and notes, and the weigh-in.
2. Decide each exercise: Keep, Step up, or Step back, by "When to step up" in the training system and my notes. No notes means Keep. If a call depends on something I haven't said, like whether I hit the top of the range, ask me once, covering every exercise in question.
3. Write the week's review: `calls` for the exercises stepping up or back, and a one-line `conclusion`.
4. Make next week's workouts match the calls: rename an exercise that moves to a heavier weight or a new variation, and adjust targets. Leave later weeks alone.
5. Tell me in a few sentences: how many workouts I finished, my weight against the goal pace, and what changes next week.

## Change the plan

Things like moving a workout, swapping an exercise I can't do or don't like, getting new equipment, traveling, or getting hurt.

* Change only upcoming workouts. A swap works the same muscles at a similar difficulty.
* For pain or an injury, follow Safety in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) first. Swap or remove what hurts.
* If the change is for good, like new equipment or a new schedule, update my [profile](../context/profile.md), and the training system if the rules change.
* Tell me what changed in a line or two.
