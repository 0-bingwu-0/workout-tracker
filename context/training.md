# How I Train

Where I train, with what, and when. Read this before planning my workouts. Below is an example setup. Say "Set me up." in Chat to replace it with yours, or edit it directly.

## Where

At home, mostly with bodyweight. No gym.

## Equipment

* 2.5 kg and 3.5 kg dumbbells, eight of each. 3.5 kg is the most I can hold in one hand.
* A pull-up bar.
* A sit-up bench.
* Resistance bands, optional.

## Schedule

* 4 strength days a week: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday.
* About 40 minutes each time.
* Walks on rest days.
