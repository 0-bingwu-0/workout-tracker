# Training System

These rules stay the same from month to month. Each month's workouts in the tracker follow them and don't repeat them. They're built around the goal, equipment, and schedule in my [profile](profile.md).

## 1. The four workouts

| Workout | Main exercises | Day | Gap before the same muscles again |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A Push + Core | Push-ups, dumbbell shoulder press, sit-ups | Mon | Next on Saturday (D has push-ups), 5 days |
| B Legs | Goblet squats, dumbbell Romanian deadlifts, lunges | Tue | Next on Saturday (D has squats), 4 days |
| C Pull + Core | Pull-ups (slow negatives or band-assisted), one-arm dumbbell rows, decline leg raises on the sit-up bench | Thu | Next Thursday, 7 days |
| D Full Body (Light) | Push-ups, dumbbell squats, dumbbell overhead press, sit-ups, 2 sets each | Sat | Next is Monday (A), 2 days, so keep it light |

The week:

| Day | Plan | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mon | A Push + Core | Back to back with Tuesday |
| Tue | B Legs | Wednesday is a rest day |
| Wed | Rest: walk 20–30 min | Recovery |
| Thu | C Pull + Core | Friday is a rest day |
| Fri | Rest: walk or full rest | Recovery |
| Sat | D Full Body (Light) | Sunday is a rest day |
| Sun | Full rest | Recover for Monday |

## 2. Rest

* 60–90 seconds between sets.
* At least 48 hours before working the same muscles hard again.
* No more than 2 training days in a row, then a rest day.
* Saturday is light and Sunday is full rest, so Monday starts fresh.

## 3. When to step up

* When all 3 sets of an exercise hit the top of this month's rep range, step it up the following week.
* End each set with 1–2 reps left. Don't go to failure on every set.
* Good form comes before reps. If form slips, do fewer reps.

Dumbbell progression (3.5 kg max per hand):

1. Add reps within the range.
2. When every set hits the top of the range and the last set still has 2 or more reps left, go heavier the next week (2.5 kg → 3.5 kg).
3. After going heavier, drop back to the bottom of the range.
4. When 3.5 kg hits the top of the range again, slow down the lowering (3 seconds).
5. If that's still easy, switch to a harder variation. One-arm rows already work one side at a time, so they go straight to step 4.

Don't step up by adding sets. D stays at 2 sets per exercise.

## 4. Each session

| Step | Time | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Warm-up | 5–8 min | Brisk walk, arm circles, hip circles, then 1 light set of the first exercise |
| 2. Main workout | About 25–35 min | The day's exercises in order, 60–90 seconds between sets |
| 3. Cool-down | 5 min | Stretch, mostly the front of the thighs, chest, and shoulders |

When you're done, mark the workout Done in the tracker. No need to log every set.

## 5. Cardio and daily life

* **Cardio:** brisk walk or easy jog 3–4 times a week, 20–30 minutes each, at a pace where you can talk but not sing. Rest days work well for it.
* **Food:** keep total calories in check, eat about 1.2–1.6 g of protein per kg of body weight (about 88–117 g a day at 73 kg), and cut back on sugary drinks and refined carbs.
* **Sleep:** 7 hours or more a night.

## 6. Logging and adjusting

Weigh in once a week, on Sunday morning, before eating and after using the bathroom. Weight moves around within a month, so watch the trend, not a single reading. Measure your waist now and then, and put it in that weigh-in's note.

1. After each workout: mark it Done in the tracker. If something stood out, add a line to its notes.
2. Every Sunday: log your weight and see how much of the week you finished.
3. After that: decide each exercise for next week. If training feels hard and your weight has stalled, look at food and sleep first before adding more.
4. Once next week's exercises and reps are set, train to those reps.

Each month's plan sets the split, rep ranges, and cardio for the month. The rep ranges for weeks 2–4 are options, not fixed.

* Hit the top of the range with plenty left: move to the next level.
* Didn't hit it: keep last week's reps.
* Pain, or clearly not recovering: step back one level.
* To step an exercise up or back, change it under Exercise changes in that week's review, and make next week's workouts match. Everything else stays as planned.
* At the end of the month, next month is planned from the last week, with the exercise changes applied to the reps.
